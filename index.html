How to play

A population of cars trains itself to drive. Each generation, the best brain is kept and mutated copies form the next generation.

Tips

  • Use Speed 5× / 10× to fast-forward training.
  • If the population stops improving, the mutation rate auto-increases.
  • Drag-and-drop a brain JSON anywhere to load it.
  • Changing sensor count resets the brain (architecture must match).
