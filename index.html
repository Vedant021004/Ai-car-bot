ACADEMIC CAPSTONE DEFENSE SUITE

EvoSafe: Safety-Aware Neuroevolution for Autonomous Driving

Third-Year Undergraduate Capstone Project · Computer Science & AI / Robotics

1. Problem Formulation & Academic Thesis

Classical autonomous driving neuroevolution relies on (1+λ) greedy hill-climbing and scalar progress heuristics (Distance + 5 · Speed). While capable of straight-line acceleration, it suffers from two critical research failures:

  • Catastrophic Diversity Collapse: Cloning a single mutated champion causes population genetic diversity to collapse below 𝒟 = 0.08, trapping vehicles in local collision-prone minima.
  • Absence of Explicit Safety Quantification: Forward-only reward formulations encourage high-speed reckless collisions, tailgating, and shoulder-line scraping.

2. Mathematical Formulation: Multi-Objective Safety Fitness

F_{safe} = w_d · d_{progress} + w_v · v̄ - w_c · 𝕀_{crash} - w_{near} · N_{near} - w_{lane} · Ē_{lane} - w_{idle} · T_{idle}
Distance Weight (w_d)1.0 (Reward for forward route progression)
Velocity Weight (w_v)3.0 (Ensures smooth, non-stagnant highway cruise)
Collision Penalty (w_c)120.0 (Severe penalty while preserving ranking gradients)
Near-Miss Weight (w_near)5.0 (Penalizes unsafe following distance / LiDAR proximity < 20%)
Lane Centering (w_lane)0.15 (Penalizes lateral deviation from lane centers)
Idle Penalty (w_idle)0.2 (Penalizes low-speed stagnation / blocking)

4. College Viva Defense: Key Examiner Questions & Answers

Q1: Why choose Genetic Algorithms over Reinforcement Learning (DQN / PPO) for this capstone?
Answer: Deep RL requires dense, differentiable reward gradients and frequently suffers from catastrophic policy forgetting when high-penalty collision states are sparse. Neuroevolution is derivative-free, works natively with non-differentiable discrete step activations, and explores non-convex parameter spaces through crossover without gradient vanishing.
Q2: How does Uniform Crossover prevent topology disruption in fixed-architecture networks?
Answer: Since all network instances maintain an identical topology ([5 inputs, 6 hidden neurons, 4 binary outputs]), uniform crossover conducts gene-for-gene synaptic recombination with 50% probability per weight matrix entry. This recombines behavioral traits (e.g. steering left around traffic from Parent A and forward acceleration from Parent B) without dimensional mismatch.
Q3: How do you address the Sim-to-Real gap in sensor perception?
Answer: Real-world LiDAR sensors exhibit physical measurement noise. We implemented a Sim-to-Real Gaussian noise injector (0-20%) on sensory inputs. Even under 10% sensory noise, EvoSafe's multi-objective safety buffer prevents catastrophic failure, demonstrating robust generalization.
Q4: What is the Explainable AI (XAI) feature attribution mechanism?
Answer: The Neural Topology Dock calculates real-time forward synaptic activation weights for each sensory ray. Saliency bars display feature attribution showing precisely which obstacle ray triggered the vehicle's avoidance steer or brake actuation.
